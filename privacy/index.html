Productivity Race

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Productivity Race is a to-do app that turns your tasks into a weekly race between categories.

Nothing leaves your device.

The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (tasks, categories, scores, streaks and settings) is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage.

The home-screen and lock-screen widgets read a small copy of your streak, race leader and incomplete to-dos from storage shared only between the app and its own widgets. To keep task names off your lock screen, choose:

Settings→Lock screen→Count only

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional)Used only for the reminders you switch on in Settings: the daily streak reminder, evening nudges for unfinished daily tasks, deadline reminders, race results and, on Android, an optional pinned list of your to-dos. All are scheduled on your device; no notification server is involved.
  • Photos (only when you tap Save to Photos)The app adds your breakdown picture to your photo library. It cannot see or read your other photos.
  • Purchases (only if you buy Unlock everything)Payment is handled entirely by Apple. The app only asks Apple whether your Apple ID owns the unlock; it never sees your payment details.
  • Sharing (only when you tap Share)The app creates a picture of your breakdown and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Reset everything to erase tasks and scores, or delete the app to remove all of its data.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
