Common questions

How do points work?

Every task you tick off moves its category forward. Quick tasks score 1 point, medium activities 3 and big commitments 5. When the race ends (each day, week or month, as you choose in Settings), the category furthest ahead wins.

How do I unlock more races and characters?

New races unlock as your daily streak grows, and new characters unlock as you complete more tasks. Or unlock every race and character at once with a one-off purchase in Settings→Unlock everything.

I bought Unlock everything. How do I get it on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, then tap Settings→Unlock everything→Restore purchase. The purchase is linked to your Apple ID, so you never pay twice.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

How do I put my to-dos on my lock screen?

On iPhone with iOS 16 or later, long-press your lock screen, tap Customise, then Lock Screen. Tap the box under the clock and choose Productivity Race, then To-dos. To keep task names private, choose Settings→Lock screen→Count only.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on in Settings→Allow notifications, and in your iPhone's Settings app under Notifications, Productivity Race. Evening nudges only appear for daily tasks you haven't ticked off yet.

Will my tasks move to a new phone?

Your data is stored on your phone and included in your iPhone backup. Restoring that backup to a new phone brings it across. The app has no account or server, so there is nothing to sign in to.

How do I start again from scratch?

Tap Settings→Reset everything to erase your tasks and scores. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Can I change the language?

The app follows your phone's language, and you can pick English, Spanish, French, German or Chinese in Settings.
